\section{Data Controller}

Pursuant to Articles 4 and 24 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Data Controller is the legal representative of the PoliNetwork APS association, namely its President.

The association has not appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO), as the conditions set out in Article 37 of the GDPR do not apply.

\textbf{Contact details for exercising privacy rights:} \\
\textbf{Email address:} \href{mailto:privacy@polinetwork.org}{privacy@polinetwork.org} (alternatively, \href{mailto:direttivo@polinetwork.org}{direttivo@polinetwork.org}) \\
\textbf{Certified email (PEC):} \href{mailto:polinetwork@pec.it}{polinetwork@pec.it}

\section{Types of data collected}

Through the volunteer application form, we collect and process the following personal data:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Identification and contact data:} first name, last name, institutional email address provided by the Politecnico di Milano, username set on the Telegram platform, telephone number, LinkedIn username, and preferred contact channel.
	\item \textbf{Academic data:} year of study, degree programme, and campus of the Politecnico di Milano.
	\item \textbf{Curricular and motivational data:} previous or current experience in other student associations, areas of interest for contributing to the association (IT, Design, Social Media, HR, etc.), reasons for applying, and links to portfolios or GitHub repositories provided voluntarily.
	\item \textbf{Statistical data:} the channel through which the applicant became aware of the form.
\end{itemize}

\section{Purposes and legal basis for processing}

Personal data will be processed exclusively for the following purposes:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item management of applications and of the selection process for new volunteers;
	\item communications with applicants concerning the ongoing or future selection process;
	\item possible establishment of a collaboration relationship or admission to the association.
\end{enumerate}
The legal basis for processing is the applicant's explicit consent, given at the same time as the application form is submitted (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

The provision of data is optional. However, failure to provide the data marked as mandatory in the form will make it impossible to assess the application. Failure to provide optional data (e.g. a link to a portfolio or LinkedIn profile) has no consequences for the selection procedure.

\section{Methods of processing}

Personal data are processed using manual, electronic, and telematic means. The procedures applied are strictly related to the purposes themselves and are implemented so as to ensure the highest level of data security and confidentiality, preventing data loss, unlawful use, and unauthorised access.

\section{Data retention}

Personal data will be retained for the period strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which they were collected. Specifically:
\begin{itemize}
	\item data will be retained in active form for the entire duration of the selection process;
	\item for \textbf{unsuccessful} applicants: at the end of the selection process, essential data concerning the outcome and progress of the application may be retained for a maximum period of \textbf{two years}, solely in order to assess any subsequent applications by the same person and to ensure the consistency of the selection process over time. At the end of this period, the data will be deleted or made fully anonymous;
	\item for \textbf{successful} applicants who join the association: the data will be included in the volunteer's record and processed in accordance with the specific privacy notice for members of the association, which will be provided upon joining.
\end{itemize}

\section{Recipients and access to data}

Personal data will not be disclosed to third parties outside the association or made publicly available. The data may be accessible exclusively to members of the PoliNetwork APS association directly involved in the selection process (for example, members of the HR area and representatives of the technical areas of interest to the applicant), who will process the data in full compliance with the internal confidentiality guidelines.

The parties that provide services for the management of the Data Controller's IT systems may also have access to the data, acting as data processors pursuant to Article 28 of the GDPR. In particular, \textbf{Microsoft Forms}, provided by Microsoft Corporation, is used to collect applications.

\textbf{Transfer of data to third countries.} Microsoft Corporation may process personal data in the United States of America. This transfer takes place in compliance with the GDPR on the basis of the following safeguards:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Microsoft's participation in the \textbf{EU--US Data Privacy Framework (DPF)}, adopted by the European Commission through its adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (pursuant to Article 45 GDPR). The list of organisations participating in the DPF can be consulted on the \href{https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov}{website};
	\item the conclusion of \textbf{Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)} approved by the European Commission (pursuant to Article 46 GDPR), which Microsoft incorporates into its contractual terms as an additional safeguard.
\end{itemize}
For further information on how Microsoft processes data, please refer to its privacy notice, available at \href{https://privacy.microsoft.com}{privacy.microsoft.com}.

\section{Rights of data subjects}

Data subjects may exercise their rights as set out in Articles 15--22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 by contacting the Data Controller using the contact details provided in Section 1 (``Data Controller'') of this document.

Specifically, data subjects have the right, at any time, to:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Right of access (Article 15):} obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning them are being processed, access those data, and receive a copy thereof;
	\item \textbf{Right to rectification (Article 16):} request the updating or rectification of inaccurate data, or the completion of incomplete data;
	\item \textbf{Right to erasure or ``right to be forgotten'' (Article 17):} obtain the erasure of their data where they are no longer necessary for the purposes for which they were collected, or where consent is withdrawn;
	\item \textbf{Right to restriction of processing (Article 18):} request that data be retained only (and not otherwise processed) in certain circumstances provided for by law;
	\item \textbf{Right to data portability (Article 20):} receive the personal data provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and, where technically feasible, transmit them to another controller without hindrance;
	\item \textbf{Right to object (Article 21):} object, in whole or in part, on grounds relating to their particular situation, to the processing of personal data concerning them.
\end{itemize}

Please also note that the selection process \textbf{is not subject to decisions based solely on automated processing}, including profiling, which produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect the individual (Article 22 GDPR). Decisions concerning applications are always made by natural persons.

Without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy, if the applicant considers that the processing of data infringes the GDPR, they have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority pursuant to Article 77 GDPR (\href{https://www.garanteprivacy.it}{www.garanteprivacy.it}).

Finally, in accordance with Article 7(3) and Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, data subjects have the right to withdraw their consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
